# Scroll — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 2026_

## The short version

Scroll has no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything it
remembers about you stays on your device.

## What's stored on your device

Saved locally, and only locally:

- **My List** — the titles you've saved.
- **Continue Watching** — which episode you reached and how far into it.
- **Recent searches** — the terms you've searched for.
- **Settings** — catalogue language, autoplay-next, start-muted.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, removes all of it. None of it is
uploaded anywhere.

## What leaves your device

Scroll is a client for a third-party catalogue. To show you anything, it
makes requests to:

- **`reelshort.vercel.app`** — the catalogue API (feeds, search, episode lists).
- **`crazymaplestudios.com`** — the CDN serving cover art and video streams.

Those requests carry what any HTTP request carries: your IP address and the
title or search term being requested. They do not carry an identifier for you,
because the app doesn't have one. How those services handle their logs is
governed by their own policies, not this one.

## What's never collected

No account details, no contacts, no location, no advertising identifiers, no
device fingerprinting, no crash or usage telemetry.

## Content

Scroll hosts no video. All titles, artwork, and streams belong to their
respective owners and are served by the third-party API above.

## Permissions

The Android build requests `INTERNET` only.
